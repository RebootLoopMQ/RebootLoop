# Phase 2 five-screen journey

The public app also has visible sections for the problem evidence, illustrative carbon scenario, industry status and privacy. These are accessible without completing an assessment.

| Screen | What happens | What it proves or does not prove |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Start | Relatable old-phone prompt and a single assessment action; boundaries and no-account promise are visible. | A public entry point, not device intake. |
| 2. Assess | Seven groups cover ownership, category, power, physical safety, condition, goal and data sensitivity. Live-demo examples can prefill, and all groups are required. | No owner, IMEI, serial number, credentials or files are requested. |
| 3. Route | Deterministic recommendation, reasons, answer summary and a next-action panel. Phone recycling links to MobileMuster; eligible computer recycling links to NTCRS; other items require accepted-item checks. | An external public link is not a partnership, booking or completed handover. |
| 4. Prepare | Route-specific self-declared checklist for ordinary working devices, or safe escalation actions and no self-erasure checkbox for stops/specialist routes. | A tick is not proof of data destruction. A repair checklist is preparation for service, not an erase. |
| 5. Passport | Minimal local record (random ID, category, route, date, Guided or Self-declared), downloadable JSON or print. In-tab counts show assessments and records, with verified outcomes at zero. | No registry, QR verification, owner proof, carbon savings or completed circular outcome. |

## Two short showcase examples

1. **Working owned iPhone, everyday data, intended for donation.** Route: prepare then donate. The recipient must be confirmed by the user. Show Apple's current guidance, tick only what was actually completed, generate a Self-declared local record. The app cannot confirm donation or a displaced purchase.
2. **Owned Windows laptop, no power, highly sensitive data, intended for recycling.** Route: professional data review before handover. No reset checklist is offered. A Guided record may show the recommendation, but the app does not promise destruction or recycling.

Other useful cases: university-managed laptop → owner/IT stop; swollen Android phone → physical-hazard stop before any normal handling; standalone SSD/USB → media-specific escalation; a good owned phone intentionally sent for recycling → public MobileMuster pathway after safe preparation.
