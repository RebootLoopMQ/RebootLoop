# MQ Discovery Pilot Plan (20–50 devices)

This is the proposed first real-world step beyond the competition prototype — a small, low-risk pilot at Macquarie University before any multi-campus expansion.

## Potential partners

| Partner | Role |
|---|---|
| MQ Sustainability & Facilities | Hosts the collection point, campus communications, outcome reporting |
| MQ IT Service Desk / Cyber Security team | Reviews guidance wording; handles the "return to IT" stop path for managed devices |
| Student re-commerce or repair club | Handles the repair/reuse route for working devices |
| Existing recycling schemes (MobileMuster, NTCRS/TechCollect) | Downstream recycling endpoint — RebootLoop routes *into* these, not around them |
| Campus charity / donation drive | Endpoint for the donate route |

## Required resources

- A pop-up table/booth for one or two days (student hub or library forecourt)
- 2–3 volunteer students to run the booth and walk participants through the flow
- Printed one-page fallback checklists for anyone without a smartphone
- A simple intake log recording only category, route and assurance state — no personal or device-identifying data
- Signage clearly stating what RebootLoop does and does not do (no erasure claims)

## Likely costs

- Printing (checklists, signage): low
- Booth/table hire: likely free if co-hosted through MQ Sustainability
- Volunteer time: unpaid/in-kind
- No hosting cost — same static/browser prototype, not a live service yet

## Risks and limitations

- Assurance remains **self-declared** for most devices unless a real destruction partner is confirmed in advance
- Turnout risk: 20–50 devices in a short window depends on promotion lead time
- Damaged/hazardous batteries turning up at the booth need a clear, rehearsed "do not handle, refer to X" process defined *before* the pilot
- Small sample size limits confidence in any reported reuse/repair rate or confidence-change metric
- Requires MQ privacy/security sign-off before logging even minimal data

## Path to scaling beyond MQ

- Routing logic, screen flow and passport schema stay fixed — only the local partner list and accepted-item rules change per campus
- Each new site runs its own small discovery pilot first, the same way MQ would
- Versioned guidance content (with an owner and review date) means updates don't need to be rebuilt per campus
