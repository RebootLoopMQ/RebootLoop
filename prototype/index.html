      Why this matters

      The device is physical. The hesitation is personal.

      It is reasonable to worry about photos, messages and account access. Knowing how to prepare a device can help someone decide what to do with it. Responsible collection also keeps hazardous materials out of inappropriate disposal pathways; the ecological effect of one device cannot be measured from a web assessment.

      Carbon scenario · one reference model

      What could a genuinely displaced new purchase mean?

      Apple reports a 56 kg CO₂e life cycle footprint for an iPhone 16 128GB, with 80% attributed to production. The reference production portion is 56 × 0.80 = 44.8 kg CO₂e. This is a comparison for one specific model, not the footprint of your device.

      Read Apple's product environmental report, p. 10 ↗
      0 kg CO₂eNo displacement assumed. An assessment or recycling decision alone does not avoid a new purchase.

      Scenario arithmetic only. It excludes repair and reuse emissions, differences between phones, rebound effects and proof of a changed purchase. RebootLoop has verified 0 kg CO₂e savings.

      For the BIT Industry Showcase · 8 October 2026

      A decision layer before handover.

      RebootLoop gives a person an informed route and secure-preparation guidance, then points them to established services where the category fits. We are a three-person Macquarie University student team. We placed fifth of 24 at Pitch for the Planet with an A1 poster and interactive proof-of-concept; this public version is the next test, not a running collection service.

      Now

      Public self-service

      Run the route, read linked official guidance, and save a local self-declared or Guided record. No device intake or booking.

      Next

      Test with real people

      Observe whether students choose the right route, understand the warning, find the destination and can explain the limits. Report actual counts and failures.

      Later

      Small campus pilot

      Only after campus permission, privacy review and an agreed handover process. Track completed outcomes separately from recommendations.

      Business model to test

      Potential buyer: a university or organisation seeking student engagement, safe guidance and evidence of completed reuse or recycling. We would test an institution-funded setup and annual licence for maintained local pathways and de-identified outcome reporting. We have no customers, revenue, unit economics or pricing evidence yet. A future pickup path would first need a verified provider, accepted-item rules, consent and a documented chain of custody. No pickup is bookable in this app.

      What we would ask an industry representative: help us test the route with users, review our safety and privacy boundaries, and discuss whether a small approved campus pilot has a real owner and budget.

      Privacy and evidence

      What this page knows, and what it cannot prove.

      Assessment answers and in-tab counts are processed in your browser memory. RebootLoop does not ask for identity, serial number, IMEI, credentials or files; it does not transmit assessment answers to a RebootLoop server. GitHub Pages and linked third-party sites may process ordinary access information under their own policies. A downloaded or printed record stays with you unless you choose to share it.

      Source dates, assumptions and known limits are documented in the evidence note. Routing is guidance, not a data erasure service, collection confirmation, third-party endorsement or measured environmental result.
